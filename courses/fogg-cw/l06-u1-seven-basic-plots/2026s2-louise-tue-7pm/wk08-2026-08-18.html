回顾 Dan Harmon 的「故事圈」八步

00:05 – 00:09
要点
  • Louise 打开「故事圈(story circle)」图,说明这是编剧 Dan Harmon 设计的、用来画出一个故事怎么推进的圆环,整门课一直在跟着它走
  • 她讲 protagonist(主角/英雄=主要人物,故事讲的就是这个人)和 antagonist——先问学生能不能猜,Kevin 试着回答;Louise 点明 antagonist 就是反派、与英雄作对的人
  • Louise 逐步讲完故事圈:①确立主角 → ②他有个「不对劲/想要或需要的东西」→ ③跨过门槛去解决问题 → ④一路上遇到人、走进危险的岔路(road of trials,可能被骗、追错东西)→ ⑤拿到他需要、并一直在找的那样东西 → ⑥为此付出代价(变老、变累、发生了某些事)→ ⑦带着它回去 → ⑧他变了(哪怕人没去任何地方,内在也认清了什么、被迫改变、接受了什么)
  • Louise 强调:这个故事圈几乎是「所有故事的秘密」,也是这门课一直在跟的东西
💡 这节在教什么
  • 这节是全课的理论骨架:用 Dan Harmon 的八步故事圈把「一个故事怎么走」讲清楚。它其实是《七个基本情节》这门课的通用底层——不管你写 rags-to-riches(白手起家)、quest(追寻)还是别的,底下的圆环是一样的。
  • Louise 教两个关键术语时先提问再给答案(protagonist / antagonist),让孩子先猜再确认,而不是直接灌。最重要的一句是第 8 步:主角的改变可以是内在的——「他可能哪儿都没去,还在原地,但内在经历了什么,认清了自己身上的某样东西」。这把「冒险 = 换地方」的误解纠正成「冒险 = 内在转变」。
  • 如果你是外人、想自己练:拿你正在写的故事,逐条填这八步——尤其第 2 步(想要什么)和第 8 步(结尾他/她哪里变了)。这两步撑不起来,故事就散。

Louise · 故事圈八步

First you think about the hero, the protagonist, then think — something isn't right, there's something they need, or something they want. And then you think, how do they find it? So they cross over into trying to solve the problem of what I want, or what I need.先想英雄、也就是主角;再想——有什么不对劲,他需要某样东西,或者想要某样东西。然后想:他怎么去找到它?于是他跨过界线,去着手解决「我想要什么、我需要什么」这个问题。 Through crossing this threshold, they start meeting other people, or looking for the solution, and the solution can lead them into dangerous rabbit holes. They may not find it straight away. They might come across people who are trying to trick them, or they may chase after the wrong thing. So the search can be a road of trials.跨过这道门槛后,他开始遇到别人、开始寻找解决办法,而这个办法会把他带进危险的岔路(兔子洞)。他不一定马上就找到。他可能遇到想骗他的人,或者追错了东西。所以这段寻找,可以是一条「历练之路(road of trials)」。 Section 5 is where they get the thing that they needed and went looking for. Then, often, they've paid a price for finding it. They've had to go through these trials, and now they're older, or tired, or something's happened to them in order to find what they need.第 5 步,是他终于拿到了他需要、也一直在找的那样东西。然后,通常他为此付出了代价。他不得不经历这些历练,现在他变老了,或者累了,或者为了得到所需的东西,他身上发生了某些事。 They then go back, and when they go back, they have changed. This journey has made them change. And this journey could be that they haven't actually gone anywhere — they're still in the same place. But they've gone through something internally, they've recognised something in themselves, they've had to change to accept things, and so they're different. That story circle is really the secret to all stories.然后他回去,而当他回去时,他已经变了。这段旅程让他改变了。而且这段旅程也可能是——他其实哪儿都没去,还在原来的地方。但他在内心里经历了什么,认清了自己身上的某样东西,不得不改变去接受一些事,所以他不一样了。这个故事圈,真的就是所有故事的秘密。

要点

  • 这是全课的核心教学法:读原文 + 逐句拆解「为什么这句有效」。Louise 不泛泛说「写得好」,而是点名具体手法——氛围(rain tapped against the window)、以细节定位(小房子、八楼)、拟人(一小时没动的笔),让表扬变成可复制的技术。

专业课程评析 · 以世界级课程分析师的视角

课后

这堂课好不好?

A quietly excellent lesson. Its strength is that everything serves one clear arc: revisit the story circle, then turn theory into a single small action (write the opening paragraph), then read every child’s work aloud and dissect exactly why each opening works. Four openings, four different strategies — atmosphere (Melody), raw desire (Emma), Dickensian contrast (Arif), obsessive comedy (Kevin) — and Louise names the technique in each. That is real teaching, not praise.一堂安静的好课。它的优点是所有环节都服务于一条清晰的主线:回顾故事圈 → 把理论落成一个极小的动作(写开头段)→ 逐个朗读每个孩子的作品、精确拆解每个开头为什么有效。四个开头、四种策略——氛围(Melody)、强烈欲望(Emma)、狄更斯式对比(Arif)、执念式喜剧(Kevin)——Louise 都点出了背后的手法。这是真正的教学,而不是夸奖。

到底教了什么?

One transferable skill: how to open a story. Concretely — put the character, the place, and the want/need into the first paragraph, and choose a hook (mood, desire, or contrast). Underneath sits the eight-step story circle, and the whole lesson quietly advances along it: paragraph 1 = establish + want (steps 1–2), the in-class second paragraph = cross the threshold (step 3), homework = meet a trial (step 4).一个可迁移的技能:如何开启一个故事。具体说——把「人物、地点、想要/需要」写进第一段,并选一个抓手(氛围、欲望或对比)。底层是八步故事圈,整堂课悄悄沿着它推进:第一段=确立人物+欲望(第 1–2 步),课上第二段=跨过门槛(第 3 步),作业=遭遇历练(第 4 步)。

哪里不好 / 哪里可以做得更好?

Three concrete misses. (1) The story-circle demo collapsed. Louise admitted “I don’t really get these lines” about her own example and abandoned it — so the class’s single worked example of applying the circle was lost, and Kevin’s brilliant Alex Rider connection (a real villain who “went into the forest at 11”) was waved away with “oh, right, okay” instead of being caught and used as the demo. A better move: drop the pre-written example and build the circle live around Kevin’s Yassin Gregorovich, turning a dead moment into the best teaching of the hour.三个具体的不足。(1)故事圈的示范塌了。Louise 对着自己的范例承认「这几行我不太懂」,然后放弃了——于是全班唯一一次「把故事圈套到具体例子上」的机会没了;而 Kevin 那个绝佳的《Alex Rider》联想(一个 11 岁就进了森林的真实反派)只被一句「哦,好,好」带过,没被接住、没被拿来当示范。更好的做法:丢掉写死的范例,就地用 Kevin 的 Yassin Gregorovich 现场把故事圈搭出来,把一个冷场变成全课最好的教学。 (2) Feedback time was lopsided. Melody, Emma and Kevin got rich, sentence-by-sentence attention; Emma’s was strong but shorter, and once the grammar quiz began, the individual writing coaching stopped — nobody’s in-class second paragraph was read or critiqued before it became homework. (3) The grammar quiz ran on broken software (the skip-timer button kept failing, colour options glitched), eating several minutes; a printed or pre-tested set of five sentences would have kept the pace Louise clearly wanted.(2)反馈时间不均衡。Melody、Emma、Kevin 得到了逐句的细致点评;Emma 的虽好但偏短;而语法小测一开始,针对个人的写作辅导就停了——没有一个人课上写的第二段被当场朗读或点评,就直接变成了作业。(3)语法小测跑在有故障的软件上(跳过计时器的按钮一直失灵、颜色选项也出错),吃掉了好几分钟;若换成一组事先测好的、印好的五个句子,就能保住 Louise 本想要的节奏。

我怎样才能学会?

Write four different openings to the same premise, each with a different hook: one that opens on atmosphere, one on a burning desire, one on character-vs-setting contrast, one that’s deliberately comic. Then label every sentence with its job (locate? mood? character? want?). Sentences you can’t label are usually the ones to cut.对同一个设定写四个不同的开头,每个用一个不同的抓手:一个从氛围切入,一个从燃烧的欲望切入,一个从「人物 vs 环境」的对比切入,一个刻意写成喜剧。然后给每一句标注它的职责(定位?氛围?人物?欲望?)。标不出职责的句子,通常就是该删的句子。

能应用在哪?

Any first page — a short story, a novel, even the first line of an essay or an email that needs to grab attention. The move “show who, where, and what they want, and pick one hook” is how professionals earn a reader’s trust in the opening lines.任何「第一页」——短篇、长篇,甚至一篇作文或一封需要抓住注意力的邮件的第一句。「点明是谁、在哪、想要什么,并选一个抓手」这个动作,正是专业作者在开头几行就赢得读者信任的方式。

设计逻辑

Recap the framework (story circle) → shrink it to one doable action (opening paragraph) → make the work visible by reading it aloud → dissect why it works → insert a brain-break (grammar) → push one step further along the circle (second paragraph / homework). Theory, tiny action, shared feedback, and forward momentum, all in one hour.回顾框架(故事圈)→ 收缩成一个能做的动作(开头段)→ 通过朗读让作品「被看见」→ 拆解它为什么有效 → 插入一次大脑休息(语法)→ 沿着故事圈再往前推一步(第二段/作业)。理论、极小动作、共读反馈、向前的推力,全都装进一小时。

一句话总结

“Describe your character, where they are, and what they need or want — and don’t go farther than that.”「描写你的人物、他在哪里、他需要或想要什么——别写得比这更远。」把一整个故事,收缩成一个人人都做得到的第一步。

留给你的一步:Pick a character. In one paragraph, put them somewhere specific, give them one burning want, and choose a single hook to open on. Stop at the end of that paragraph — then label each sentence’s job.挑一个人物。用一段话,把他放进一个具体的地点,给他一个燃烧的渴望,并选一个抓手开场。写到这一段结束就停——然后给每句话标注它的职责。
